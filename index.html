SECURITY REPORT FORMAT

How I organise assessment findings

01. Finding: A short title describing the observation.

02. Scope: The authorised system or application assessed.

03. Evidence: Relevant screenshots, logs, or safe test results.

04. Risk: A clear explanation of potential impact.

05. Recommendation: Practical steps to improve security.

06. Status: Open, resolved, or awaiting verification.
